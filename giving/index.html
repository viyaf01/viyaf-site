Practical information

How are donations arranged?

Please contact office@viyaf.org. The foundation confirms documentation, necessary identity and source-of-funds checks, and account details individually. Formal commitments require joint representation by two board members. No funds are collected through personal accounts or consumer payment apps.

Does support include a performance place or other personal benefit?

A donation does not purchase a performance place, admission, title, investment return or governance rights. Paid services and sponsorship with consideration follow separate commercial agreements.

Is a donation tax-deductible?

An acknowledgement of funds received does not itself establish tax deductibility. Treatment depends on the donor’s jurisdiction and the foundation’s recognised status at the time of the gift; please confirm with your tax adviser.

Can support be provided across borders?

Cross-border arrangements are accepted only where permitted by applicable law and after the required documentation and checks. VIYAF does not solicit donations within Mainland China. No person or entity in Mainland China is authorised to collect donations on behalf of VIYAF.
