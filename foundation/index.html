VIYAF · VIENNA

The foundation

Based in Vienna, VIYAF is dedicated to young artistic talent and international cultural exchange.

Rooted in Vienna.
Open to the world.

Established in 2025, VIYAF is an Austrian private foundation. Its purpose includes supporting young talent in music and other arts, cultural exchange, artistic innovation and education.

VIYAF is a Vienna-based Austrian private foundation devoted to the development of young musical and artistic talent. Our mission connects artistic growth, professional exchange and encounters across cultures. VIYAF Virtuosi is our flagship concert brand; each project brings together the artists and organisations responsible for its presentation.

Board member · Stiftungsvorstand

Prof. Mag. Elzbieta Maria Wiedner-Zajac

A Vienna-based pianist, composer and educator, Elzbieta Maria Wiedner-Zajac contributes her artistic and educational experience to VIYAF. At the 2026 Charity Art Night she welcomed guests and took part in the young artist certificate presentation.

Project relationships

VIYAF is an Austrian private foundation. Individual project pages identify the responsible organisers and partners. Meta Management GmbH is a legally separate commercial entity and is not a subsidiary of VIYAF.

VIYAF provides the institutional brand framework for VIYAF Virtuosi. Meta Management GmbH is involved in concert promotion, production and international coordination, as identified for each project.
