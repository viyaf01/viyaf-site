Foundation Profile

About the Foundation

VIYAF is a Privatstiftung established and registered under Austria's Private Foundation Act (PSG). It pursues charitable purposes under its statutes and distributes no dividends; available funds and surpluses serve the charitable purpose.

Legal nameWiener Internationale Jugendkunstentwicklung PrivatstiftungVienna International Youth Arts Development Foundation (VIYAF)
Legal formPrivatstiftung (Austrian private foundation · non-profit)
RegistrationFN 653499 dCourt of registration: Handelsgericht Wien
Registered addressSchweidlgasse 30/2/56, 1020 Vienna, Austria
RepresentationTwo board members sign jointly (Two-Signature Rule)
PurposeSupport young musical and artistic talent; promote international cultural exchange and innovative arts projects; organise training, competitions and masterclassesAncillary asset management safeguards long-term charitable purposes

VIYAF is an independent Privatstiftung established under Austrian law. Where required for a project, the Foundation may engage qualified external delivery partners under separate written agreements. Such engagements are subject to applicable board approval, conflict-of-interest, arm's-length and documentation requirements. Meta Management GmbH is a legally separate commercial entity and is not a subsidiary of VIYAF.

Stiftungsvorstand · Board Member

Prof. Mag. Elzbieta Maria Wiedner-Zajac

International pianist · composer · educator (Vienna)

A pianist, composer and educator based in Vienna, she trained in Poland, the United States (University of Michigan, Ann Arbor) and Austria, and taught for many years at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna (mdw), where she obtained her Habilitation. She has appeared on leading European stages, including the Goldener Saal of the Vienna Musikverein, and her recordings and publications have earned high professional recognition.

She has received national honours from Austria — including the Austrian Cross of Honour for Science and Art — and from Poland. As a long-standing mdw educator and VIYAF board member, she brings stage standards, academic judgement and institutional credibility to the Foundation.

Giving & Partnership Framework

Support Levels

Public support levels are deliberately simple. Final terms follow the signed Donation Agreement and related documents; detailed project material is shared with prospective donors in structured conversations.

LevelSuggested amountPublic recognition

View the Full Giving Framework Arrange a Private Conversation

Donations are voluntary, gratuitous and irrevocable, and confer no investment return, profit share, income right, equity, debt claim, governance right or beneficiary distribution right. Donors may suggest a field or project direction, but do not purchase admission, titles, performance slots or other personal outcomes; scholarships and young-artist support are determined through an independent professional process and conflict review.

Finance & Governance

Three Separate Channels

Donations, sponsorship and program service fees use separate contracts, accounts, accounting bases and disclosures.

Donation / Spende

Entity: VIYAF Privatstiftung (Austria)

Donation Agreement / Impact Agreement. Supports young arts, scholarships, masterclasses and public-benefit projects. No investment return or income rights; tickets, dinners or other consideration are separated at fair value.

Sponsoring

Entity: Meta Management GmbH (Austria)

Sponsoring Agreement / Commercial Cooperation Agreement. Brand exposure, joint communications, client events and ESG/CSR content; commercial invoice, no donation acknowledgement. Meta Management GmbH is a legally separate commercial entity and is not a subsidiary of VIYAF.

Program Fee

China-side entity: iMusic Union (Shenzhen) Cultural Technology Co., Ltd.

Program Participation Agreement. Student participation, masterclasses, travel, teaching rehearsals and competition training — a commercial service fee, not a VIYAF donation.
