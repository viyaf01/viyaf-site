Meet VIYAF ↗

Founded in Vienna in 2025, VIYAF is an Austrian private foundation dedicated to young artistic talent and international cultural exchange.

We bring young artists into dialogue with experienced musicians, audiences and cultural partners — through performance, artistic development and exchange.
