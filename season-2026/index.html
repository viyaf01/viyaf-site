VIYAF VIRTUOSI · 2026

2026 Season

Four concerts at the Musikverein Wien, two in Arad, and a Charity Art Night in Vienna form the summer archive. The season continues in Berlin on 16 October.

Vienna ↗Arad ↗Charity Art Night ↗Berlin · 16 October ↗

Musikverein Wien · 3, 15 & 16 August 2026

Vienna

Summer archive

The Vienna programme moved between the scale of the Goldener Saal and the intimacy of the Brahms-Saal. Filarmonica Arad and conductor Martin Braun joined the concerto concerts on 15 and 16 August. The Brahms-Saal performances on 3 and 16 August added chamber music and individual artistic voices to the season.

Palace of Culture · 11–12 August 2026

Arad

Summer archive

At the Palace of Culture in Arad, the orchestra and international soloists came together for two programmes before the Vienna concerts. The photographs capture the exchange between conductor, orchestra and soloist — from piano and marimba to the vocal stage.

19:00

VIYAF Virtuosi — Great Piano Concertos I

Palatul Cultural Arad · Palace of Culture

The first Arad concert brought international soloists together with Filarmonica Arad under Martin Braun, with piano and marimba among the featured instruments.

Official project announcement · ICR Vienna ↗

VIYAF Virtuosi — Great Piano Concertos II

Palatul Cultural Arad · Palace of Culture

A second evening at the Palace of Culture continued the collaboration between Filarmonica Arad, Martin Braun and the soloists, with piano concertos and vocal performance.
